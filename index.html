Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

We collect information necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email, organization, and billing details when you sign up for our services.
  • Usage Data: API calls, feature interactions, and service performance metrics to improve our platform.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device OS, and approximate location for security and analytics purposes.
  • Communication Data: Messages, support tickets, and feedback you share with our team.

2. Usage of Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our software consulting and AI integration services.
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Communication: To send service-related announcements and respond to your inquiries.
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and optimize user experience.
  • Compliance: To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls
  • Compliance with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA standards
  • Dedicated security incident response team

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data at any time.
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Portability: Export your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications and optional data processing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for specific data processing activities.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Specific retention periods depend on the data type and applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion of your data at any time, except where we have a legal obligation to retain it. Account data is retained for 90 days following deletion for recovery purposes.

6. Third-Party Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers (hosting, payment processing, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business partners (only with your explicit consent)

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, remember preferences, and gather analytics data. Most browsers allow you to decline cookies, but this may limit certain features. We only use strictly necessary cookies by default and require your consent for analytics and marketing cookies.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered accounts. Continued use of our services following notification of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
